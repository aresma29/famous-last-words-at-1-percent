An entry for the make.fun micro-competition **Famous Last Words at 1 Percent**.

The entry is a phone's last words at 1 percent battery: it declares its love
for the charger, reveals it spent its final percent keeping the user's alarm
alive, and says it would do it all again — even though the battery was never
once thanked.

## Entry

> Tell the charger I loved it. I spent my last percent keeping your alarm alive, and I would do it again, even though you never once thanked the battery.

(151 characters, limit 199.)

## Run

```sh
python3 -P -s main.py
```

`main.py` is stdlib-only and resolves `entry.txt` relative to its own
location, so it runs from any working directory.
